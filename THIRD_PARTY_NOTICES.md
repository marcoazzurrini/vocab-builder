# Third-party notices

## Lexique-derived vocabulary data

The vocabulary resources contain selections and adaptations of Lexique 4.00 data by Boris New, Christophe Pallier, and the contributors credited in the upstream README. Lexique supplies French forms, grammatical observations, frequencies, and morphological annotations. The Italian cues, selected teaching senses, family policy, curriculum ordering, and presentation notes are project additions; they are not endorsed by Lexique's authors.
